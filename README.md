# Dynamic-First-Crossing-Behavior-in-Kalshi-Sports-Markets
This project studies Kalshi sports markets using a first-crossing framework. Instead of focusing
only on terminal prices or final outcomes, the analysis examines whether and when each market’s price falls
below a low threshold of 5 cents. This approach allows us to investigate whether market odds touching
an extremely low probability during the course of a market being traded has predictive power on the final
market outcome. The goal is to understand whether low-price crossing is associated with eventual resolution
outcome and whether this behavior differs across contracts that resolve YES versus NO. In doing so, the
project evaluates longshot behavior and calibration dynamics at the level of the full price path.
